From Donor to Patient

Four steps. zero compromise.

Every unit travels through a verified chain of custody — from the donor's arm to a hospital ward — in less than thirty-six hours. Here's how it works.

01

Register & Screen

A 12-minute health questionnaire and rapid haemoglobin test confirm donor eligibility on the spot.

~12 MIN
02

Collect

Sterile single-use kits draw 450ml of whole blood while you rest. Refreshments and recovery follow.

~10 MIN
03

Test & Separate

Nucleic-acid tests for HIV, Hep B/C, syphilis & malaria. Whole blood split into red cells, plasma & platelets.

~6 HOURS
04

Dispatch

Validated 4°C transport delivers each unit to one of 312 partner hospitals — temperature logged end-to-end.

~28 HOURS
Our Mission

Carry safe blood to whoever needs it, wherever they are, before time runs out.

Blood is a perishable, living resource. Every unit moves through screening, separation, cold-chain logistics, and patient matching within hours of donation. We exist to make that pipeline reliable, ethical, and unbroken — drawing strength from a network of volunteer donors and trained medical staff who share one principle: humanity first.

We are independent, neutral, and accountable. We screen for safety. We honour the donor. We answer the hospital. We never charge the patient.

Eligibility & FAQs

Can I donate today?

A short list to help you check eligibility before you arrive. Final clearance is given by our medical officer at the centre.

  • Aged 18–60, weighing 50 kg or more
  • Haemoglobin ≥ 12.5 g/dL (tested on site)
  • At least four months since your last donation
  • Well-rested, hydrated, and free of infection
Does donating blood hurt or weaken me? +

A brief pinprick is the only sensation most donors report. Your body replaces the volume within 24 hours and the cells within a few weeks. Donors are screened for haemoglobin and blood pressure beforehand to ensure safety.
How often can I donate? +

Whole-blood donors can return every four months (every three months for men in good health). Plasma and platelet donations have shorter intervals — our medical staff will guide you.
What happens to my blood after I donate? +

Your unit is screened for five infectious markers, separated into red cells, plasma and platelets, and assigned a unique chain-of-custody barcode. It typically reaches a patient within 36 hours.
Is there a cost to the patient? +

No. Nutech Blood Bank does not charge patients for blood. Hospitals may pass on processing costs as set by national policy — we publish those rates publicly.
How do I request blood in an emergency? +

Call our 24/7 dispatch line at 0349 5614416 or use the hospital partner portal. Verified requests are matched and dispatched typically within 30 minutes inside city limits.
